PocketMOBAlert Free
A man-overboard alarm that uses the crew's own phones. No extra hardware, no internet.

Each phone sends a small Bluetooth signal and watches the others. If a crew member's phone goes out of range, the watching phones sound a loud alarm (even on silent) and save the GPS position.

Free forever: the full alarm for up to 2 paired crew phones. PocketMOBAlert Pro has unlimited crew, full alert history and a MOB waypoint for OpenCPN.

A safety aid, not a replacement for a certified AIS personal locator beacon. It only works if the crew member carries the phone.

Installing
Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Coming soon to Google Play; until then you install the APK file. Android will show an "unrecognized app" / Play Protect warning and ask you to allow installs from this source the first time. That is normal for apps installed outside Google Play.
